Rules

  1. The course is fixed. 300 m on the ski erg, then 500 m on the rower, then 10 calories on the assault bike, in that order.
  2. One clock. Time runs from the first pull on the ski erg to the moment the bike reads 10 cal. Transitions between machines and any rest count. No pausing the clock and no adding three machine times together.
  3. Video or it did not happen. One continuous video of the whole attempt, showing each monitor reaching its distance or calories and the move between machines. A cut in the video voids the attempt.
  4. Monitors reset to zero before each leg, and the reset is on the video.
  5. As many attempts as you like. Only your best time counts.
  6. Deadline. Attempts must be emailed by 5pm Monday 30 November 2026.

How to submit a time

Email Ryan McCracken with "Erg challenge" in the subject line. Put your total time in the body (for example 3:42.5) and attach the video, or a link to it if the file is too big for email. Say which day you did it if it was not the day you sent it.

Times go on the board once the video has been checked. Anything that cannot be verified from the video is not scored.
